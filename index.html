Research Interests

My research focuses on the intersection of video games and second-language acquisition. Existing research increasingly suggests that video games can support language learning. Drawing on models proposed by linguists, we can empirically and quantitatively assess gains in language proficiency through the use of either commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) games or purpose-built games.
